Bulk import template

SocialBee's importer wants six specific columns, and the first row must not be edited. Note what isn't here: there is no platform or category column. You choose the profiles and the destination category in the import screen, not in the file. Dates must be yyyy-mm-ddThh:mm:ssZ on a 24-hour clock, and any date already in the past will fail rather than publish.
